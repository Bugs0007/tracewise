## Video direction

Use the Broadside preset remixed to Tracewise's native dark paper and cobalt ink. Fonts come from the repository: Schibsted Grotesk for large type and Commit Mono for code/data. Keep mint for found/correct, mustard for pending, and tomato for comparisons. This semantic adjustment is necessary to preserve the source product's visual meaning. Typography, real diagrams, and code carry the motion. No em dashes, gradients, glowing particles, voice, music, or sound effects. Fast attacks, motivated camera changes, finite seek-safe motion, and intentional read holds. The final URL holds for at least two seconds. Main copy stays above the bottom chrome; no separate caption pill because every message is designed into the frame.
